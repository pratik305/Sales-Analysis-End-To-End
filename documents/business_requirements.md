# Business Requirements Document
## Sales & Marketing Analytics — Maven Fuzzy Factory

---

## 1. Business Context

Maven Fuzzy Factory is an e-commerce retailer selling teddy bear products through its website.  
The business runs multiple digital marketing campaigns across different channels (search, direct, social, etc.) and wants to understand how marketing efforts translate into sales, profitability, and customer behavior.

The company currently has access to detailed raw data (orders, order items, sessions, pageviews, and refunds), but lacks a centralized analytical view to support decision-making.

---

## 2. Business Problem

The business faces difficulty answering key performance questions such as:

- Which marketing channels drive the most revenue?
- How efficient are marketing campaigns in converting visitors into customers?
- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- How do refunds impact net revenue?
- How does customer behavior vary by device type and over time?

Without a structured analytics layer, stakeholders rely on ad-hoc analysis, which is time-consuming and error-prone.

---

## 3. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. Create a **single source of truth** for sales and marketing analytics.
2. Enable **executive-level visibility** into revenue, profitability, and conversion performance.
3. Analyze **marketing effectiveness** by source, campaign, and device.
4. Identify **high-performing and underperforming products**.
5. Quantify the **impact of refunds** on net sales.
6. Support **time-based analysis** (trend, YoY, YTD).

---

## 4. Key Business Questions

### Sales & Profitability
- What are total sales, costs, profit, and profit margin?
- How many orders and items are sold?
- What is net sales after refunds?

### Marketing Performance
- How many sessions are generated by each marketing source?
- What is the conversion rate by source and campaign?
- Which campaigns generate the highest revenue per session?

### Product Performance
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products have high refund rates?
- Are there products with high sales but poor profitability?

### Customer Behavior
- How does performance differ between desktop and mobile users?
- Are repeat sessions more likely to convert?

---

## 5. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder Role | Information Needs |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Executives | Overall revenue, profitability, and trends |
| Marketing Team | Channel & campaign performance, conversion efficiency |
| Product Team | Product sales, refunds, and profitability |
| Analysts | Clean data model for ad-hoc analysis |

---

## 6. Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it delivers:

- Accurate and validated KPIs:
  - Total Sales
  - Net Sales
  - Total Orders
  - Total Items Sold
  - Conversion Rate
  - Profit Margin
- Interactive dashboards that:
  - Load quickly
  - Are easy to interpret
  - Allow slicing by date, device, campaign, and product
- A scalable data model aligned with industry best practices (star schema).

---

## 7. Scope & Constraints

### In Scope
- Sales, marketing, and refund analytics
- Historical analysis using available data
- Dashboarding using Power BI

### Out of Scope
- Real-time streaming data
- Predictive modeling
- Customer lifetime value modeling
- Production deployment on cloud infrastructure

---

## 8. Assumptions

- Each row in `order_items` represents one unit sold.
- Refunds are modeled as adjustments to sales, not independent transactions.
- Data provided is accurate and complete for analytical purposes.
- Databricks Community Edition limitations apply.

---

## 9. Deliverables

- Curated analytical tables (Fact & Dimension tables)
- Power BI dashboards:
  - Executive Overview
  - Marketing Performance
- Project documentation and data dictionary

---

## 10. Expected Business Impact

By implementing this analytics solution, Maven Fuzzy Factory can:

- Make data-driven marketing budget decisions
- Identify profitable products and risky SKUs
- Improve campaign efficiency and conversion rates
- Reduce revenue leakage caused by refunds
- Establish a foundation for future advanced analytics
